Real-Time Speech Transcription

Connecting live audio to multilingual speech recognition through Sarvam AI and LiveKit.

Sarvam AI · LiveKit · Python

The problem

Applications that process conversations need transcription while speech is arriving, including speech in multiple languages.

System architecture

At Flagestrator, I integrated Sarvam AI speech-to-text with the LiveKit SDK for real-time, multilingual transcription in enterprise AI applications.

Implementation

  • Connected a real-time audio workflow to a speech-recognition integration.
  • Worked on multilingual transcription rather than an offline, single-language file demo.

Technical challenges

Streaming integrations must bridge audio delivery and model output without making the application wait for a complete recording. Transport and recognition behavior need to be considered together.
